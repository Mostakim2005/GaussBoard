## GazBoard 4.8.9

This patch addresses the Study Tape and answer-cover behaviors demonstrated in the user's recordings.

- Tap a rectangular answer cover to reveal it; tap again to conceal it. The cover remains on the board.
- Freehand Study Tape has the same persistent toggle behavior.
- Revealed outlines remain tappable, and attached content follows the cover state.
- Added a dedicated Study Tape drawing tool in the ribbon and Add/tools menus.
- Fixed freehand tape hit-test bounds and a missing renderer helper import.

Verification before release: `npm run smoke:features:4.8.8` (6/6) and `node scripts/smoke.js` (1,240/1,240). Windows installer and portable executable build results and checksums are recorded in `docs/releases/RELEASE-4.8.9.md`.
